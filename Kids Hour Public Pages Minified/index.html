Kit Clash Arena

Rules

Goal

Deal 100 damage before your opponent. The winner earns 50 coins.

Controls

Player 1 uses A/D to move, W to jump, and S to shoot. Player 2 uses the arrow keys.

Choosing a Loadout

Before a match, W or Up switches between weapon and perk selection. Press Shoot when ready.

Packs and Coins

Buy packs to unlock weapons, then perks, then Mega upgrades. Triple and Lucky packs provide special purchase options.

Combat

Every hit causes knockback. Faster projectiles push harder, while bombs use a fixed explosion knockback.

Mega Forms

Mega upgrades cost more and improve a character's powers. Gold outlining shows that Mega is active.

Modes and Arenas

Fight a friend or an Easy, Medium, or Hard bot. Maps contain platforms and hazards. Test Arena lets both players freely change weapons, perks, Mega forms, and maps without losing health.
